Privacy Policy — Trivia Duel

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Trivia Duel ("the app") is a quiz game published by Saurabh Bhatia. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.bhatias.triviaduelproject.

The short version

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. You are never asked to create an account. The only piece of information you actively give the app is a display name you choose yourself for online duels, which can be anything.

What the app collects

  • An anonymous player ID. When you play online, Firebase Authentication issues a random anonymous ID so the game can tell players in a duel apart. It is not linked to any personal identity, email, or Google account.
  • A display name you choose. Shown to the other players in your duel. You pick it; it does not have to be your real name.
  • Live game state. While an online duel is running, the room code, questions, answers, scores and streaks are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so every player sees the same game. This is game data, not personal data.
  • On-device statistics. Games played, wins, and best streak are saved only on your own device and are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may access your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to select and measure ads.

What the app does not collect

  • No real name, email address, or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera access.
  • No sign-in with Google, Facebook, or any other identity provider.

Third-party services

The app relies on these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — anonymous IDs and live duel state. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob — advertising. How Google uses data
  • Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com) — the app downloads quiz questions from this public API. No information about you is sent with these requests.

Advertising and your choices

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can choose non-personalised ads. On any device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

On-device statistics are removed when you uninstall the app. Online duel rooms are transient game records that are no longer accessible once a duel ends. Because accounts are anonymous and hold no personal information, there is no profile to delete — but if you would like a specific duel record removed, contact us at the address below and include the room code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
