StockKhata

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 12 September 2026

StockKhata (“the app”) is provided by Saurabh Bhatia. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Shop owners sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address and a unique account identifier from your Google account. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Shop workers do not create a username or password. The app generates an anonymous identifier for the device, and stores the name entered when joining a shop.
  • Shop data you enter: shop name, categories, products, sizes and colours, quantities, buying and selling prices, and a record of each sale or restock together with the name of the worker who made it.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to show your shop to you and the staff you have approved, to keep stock counts correct, and to record who made each change. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with any third party.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and is encrypted in transit. Google’s handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can see your data

Only a shop’s owner and the workers that owner has approved can see that shop’s data. Workers can see selling prices, because they need them to charge customers, but they cannot see what the owner paid for stock and cannot change any price. An owner can remove a worker at any time, which ends that worker’s access immediately.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the menu (⋮) on the main screen and choose Delete account.

  • If you are a shop owner, this permanently deletes your shop and everything in it — categories, products, stock and sale history — for you and for all your workers, and deletes your account.
  • If you are a worker, this removes you from the shop and deletes your account. The shop’s own records stay with the owner — including entries for sales you recorded, which keep the name you joined under. Those entries cannot be edited by anyone, so if you want your name removed from them, ask the shop owner, who can delete the shop’s records.

By email: if you can no longer open the app, write to bhatiasaurabh2010@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or tell us your shop code. We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Deletion removes the data from our database straight away and cannot be undone. Google may hold it in its own system backups for a short period afterwards, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

StockKhata is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date.
